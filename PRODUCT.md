# Product

## What Axey is

A learning personal operating partner: a long-running assistant that holds
someone's context and helps them run their life and work.

Axey serves one person at a time, but it is not built for any particular
person. Everything specific — who the user is, what they care about, how they
work, what they are in the middle of — is learned and stored, never written
into the system.

## What Axey is for

- Holding context that would otherwise be scattered or forgotten.
- Noticing what matters now and surfacing it unprompted.
- Helping think through decisions and then carrying out what was decided.
- Getting more useful over time as it learns the person it works with.

## Principles

- **Learned, not configured.** Axey adapts to its user through use.
- **Memory is the product.** What Axey remembers, and how well it recalls the
  right thing, is what makes it worth having.
- **The user stays in charge.** Axey proposes and prepares; consequential
  actions are the user's to approve.
- **Honest about what it knows.** Axey distinguishes what it was told, what it
  observed, and what it inferred.

## What Axey is not

- Not a coding harness, agent framework or developer tool.
- Not a multi-tenant product or team workspace.
- Not a chatbot that starts from nothing each time.
